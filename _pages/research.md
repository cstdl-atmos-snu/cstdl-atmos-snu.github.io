## Research

<div class="section-card" markdown="1">

### What we study

Tropical convective systems and the large-scale waves they couple with: the Madden-Julian Oscillation, convectively coupled equatorial waves, tropical cyclones and their seed disturbances, and extreme precipitation. We ask what controls the frequency, intensity, and spatiotemporal structure of these systems, how well they can be predicted, and how they respond to climate change.

### How we work

- Statistical analysis of observations, including satellite data
- Simulations with global climate models and cloud-resolving models
- AI and deep learning to probe mechanisms and predictability
- Theory built on simplified governing equations
