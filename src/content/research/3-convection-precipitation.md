---
title: "Tropical deep convection and extreme precipitation"
description: "The mesoscale organization of deep convective systems, the processes that set precipitation intensity, and changes in extremes with warming."
order: 3
---

We investigate tropical deep convective systems, the vertical motion inside them, mesoscale convective clustering, and the mechanisms behind extreme rain rates, using satellite observations and global storm-resolving models. Related work covers tropical cirrus, humid heatwaves, and regional extreme precipitation under emission scenarios.
