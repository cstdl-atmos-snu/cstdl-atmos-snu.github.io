---
title: "Tropical cyclones and their seeds"
description: "The disturbances from which tropical cyclones form, what makes them develop or decay, and how cyclone tracks and rapid intensification can be predicted."
order: 2
---

We track tropical cyclone seed disturbances in reanalyses and models, study why some seeds develop while others do not, and examine the environmental conditions behind rapid intensification. We also evaluate tropical cyclones in high-resolution climate models and subseasonal forecast systems.
