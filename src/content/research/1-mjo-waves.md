---
title: "The MJO and convectively coupled waves"
description: "How tropical convection organizes into the Madden-Julian Oscillation and convectively coupled equatorial waves, and what controls their propagation and predictability."
order: 1
---

We study the Madden-Julian Oscillation (MJO) and convectively coupled equatorial waves from observations, reanalyses, climate and cloud-resolving models, and theory. Current work includes MJO prediction and predictability, the representation of these phenomena in climate models, and their response to surface warming.
