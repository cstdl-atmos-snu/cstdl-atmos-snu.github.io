Daehyun Kim leads the Convective Systems/Tropical Dynamics Laboratory in the School of Earth and Environmental Sciences at Seoul National University. Before joining SNU in 2023, he worked at the University of Washington (2014–2023) and Columbia University (2010–2014). He received his B.S. and Ph.D. from Seoul National University.
